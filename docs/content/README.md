## Current state

The Society is still in formation. The phases below describe the work required to make its hypotheses real, not live services or promised launch dates.

Today:

- the Manifesto is published;
- Part A and Part B of the Founding Book are complete working drafts;
- the Charter is a draft derived from the book and has not been ratified;
- Essentia v0.1.0 is runnable research software for a narrower ledger experiment;
- Essentia v0.9.0 is the current monetary and civic research architecture, not an implemented network;
- the Academy is an early beta;
- no Freedom Floor, `𝒰`, `ℛ`, `ℰ`, civic identity, binding voting, or benefit system is live.

## Program roadmap

### Phase I - Public foundation and formal research

The first phase is already underway: publish the Society's principles, make its hypotheses inspectable, and build the models, accounting, privacy, governance, security, and verification work needed to test them.

Current priorities are:

- **Founding Book:** maintain the distinction among values, established findings, context-dependent evidence, working hypotheses, design conjectures, and unknowns.
- **Essentia monetary research:** model how `ℰ` can acquire and retain value through coordinated acceptance, equal civic issuance, useful exchange, dense trade loops, producer credit, and public monetary rules.
- **Charter development:** derive identity, governance, safety, and economy articles from Part B rather than preserving prototype assumptions.
- **Open evidence:** publish decision records, sources, assumptions, model code, pilot protocols, negative results, and corrections.
- **Academy:** test accessible learning and guidance without treating completion metrics as proof of social impact.

### Phase II - Founding market and shadow economy

Essentia should begin by coordinating a market, not by distributing isolated wallets.

A founding market brings together:

- people receiving indexed `𝒰` entitlements;
- providers quoting goods and services in `𝒰`;
- suppliers willing to accept some `ℰ` from providers;
- workers and cooperatives able to earn and spend `ℰ`;
- public or common institutions offering useful services in `ℰ`;
- producer-credit and external-liquidity partners.

Before real issuance, the complete flow should run in shadow accounting:

```text
𝒰 -> ℛ -> ℰ
```

The shadow system should measure provider reuse, trade-loop closure, shortages, price translation, external leakage, identity errors, settlement delay, and economic concentration. No participant should depend on the shadow balance for survival.

### Phase III - Outside `ℰ` issuance experiment

Test whether transparently issued outside `ℰ`, distributed equally and without a guaranteed fixed external redemption, can acquire positive internal purchasing power.

The test should include:

- simultaneous onboarding of consumers and providers;
- standing offers priced in `𝒰`;
- public equal-issuance rules;
- useful Society and cooperative services purchasable in `ℰ`;
- supplier matching and obligation clearing;
- multiple bootstrap strategies;
- removal or reduction of temporary subsidies to test whether acceptance persists.

The purpose is to discover the conditions under which shared agreement becomes a durable monetary equilibrium.

### Phase IV - Indexed `𝒰 -> ℛ -> ℰ` conversion

Issue a small real `𝒰` entitlement and let participants choose:

- direct payment to a provider;
- unrestricted conversion into `ℰ`;
- mixed settlement where a provider accepts `ℰ` plus another agreed form.

`ℛ` preserves the real claim between activation and settlement. The system should compare conversion based on internal purchasing power with conversion based on thin external markets and establish why the two rates can diverge.

Large nominal `ℰ` settlements should be evaluated in real `𝒰` terms. A neutral redenomination may keep the unit readable without changing anyone's economic position.

### Phase V - Supply-coupled issuance

Universal demand should become information and finance for expanding supply.

Use aggregated, privacy-preserving `ℛ` data to identify bottlenecks and test:

- inventory and working-capital finance;
- advance purchase commitments;
- housing and care capacity;
- local food, energy, transport, and communication systems;
- producer credit in `ℰ`;
- recruitment of external providers into partial `ℰ` acceptance;
- anti-monopoly and anti-rent-capture interventions.

The central measurement is how much new demand becomes real production rather than higher prices, longer queues, or scarcity rents.

### Phase VI - External Liquidity Bridge

Build markets and institutions that connect `ℰ` to national currencies, imports, taxes, debts, and providers outside the network.

The bridge may include reserves, revenue, credit, grants, market makers, exchanges, procurement, and payment partners. It supports interoperability and confidence. It is not one-to-one backing for every `ℰ`.

Research should measure:

- external exchange at different order sizes;
- market depth and price impact;
- coordinated cash-out;
- import dependence;
- reserve and credit use;
- divergence between internal and external purchasing power;
- whether participants continue to value `ℰ` for what it does inside the network.

### Phase VII - Recurring Freedom Floor pilot

Run a recurring `𝒰` allocation large enough to affect ordinary life.

The pilot should include universal eligibility within the defined population, direct essential payment, unrestricted conversion, provider settlement, producer finance, and transparent monetary control.

Measure real income, output, prices, shortages, rent capture, provider entry and exit, desired `ℰ` balances, seigniorage distribution, external leakage, household choices, health, stress, identity errors, appeals, and ecological effects.

At least one implementation should fund a meaningful share through outside civic issuance. The hypothesis cannot be tested if every real unit is quietly replaced by conventional funding.

### Phase VIII - Federation

Connect independently governed nodes with different costs of living, productive structures, laws, and external currencies.

Federation requires:

- portable `𝒰` rights;
- cross-node `ℰ` acceptance;
- multilateral clearing;
- local and global monetary authority boundaries;
- deficit and surplus adjustment;
- credit and contagion limits;
- external settlement rules;
- node entry, exit, and resolution;
- protection against richer nodes turning poorer nodes into permanent debt colonies.

### Phase IX - Distributed public infrastructure

The shared ledger architecture follows the demonstrated trust problem.

Possible directions include:

- signed append-only public records with independent witnesses;
- replicated clearing among independent nodes;
- Byzantine fault tolerant consensus when shared finality requires it;
- plural, purpose-bound credentials;
- privacy-preserving eligibility and voting;
- machine-assisted explanation with human authority and appeal;
- cryptographic agility and post-quantum migration;
- resilient local operation without one technical or institutional point of failure.

## Working commitments

1. **Dignity constrains power.** No person or conscious being becomes disposable because of status, capacity, conformity, or usefulness.
2. **Interdependence is unavoidable.** The practical question is whether it is organized through reciprocity and care or through dependence and domination.
3. **Stabilization, education, and repair should precede coercion when they can safely do so.** Immediate threats may still require the least restrictive effective intervention.
4. **Shared agreement can create money.** The research problem is how to coordinate that agreement around useful production, equal issuance, trustworthy rules, and durable acceptance.
5. **A large nominal mint is not a complete inflation theory.** Real claims, productive response, desired balances, velocity, distribution, expectations, and bottlenecks must be measured.
6. **The Freedom Floor targets real purchasing power.** `𝒰` defines the entitlement, `ℛ` preserves it during settlement, and `ℰ` is the common money.
7. **Progress must be observable.** Reduced suffering, expanded agency, usable income, ecological repair, accessible knowledge, and accountable power matter more than ideological purity or institutional self-preservation.

This is a proposal for an ecosystem of understanding and participation. It is not presently a government, public authority, benefit program, currency issuer, or operating monetary system.

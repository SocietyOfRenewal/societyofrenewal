## Proposed Program Roadmap

The Society is still in formation. The phases below describe possible directions,
not live services or committed launch dates. Today the Charter is a draft,
Part A of the Founding Book is drafted, Essentia is a v0.1.0 prototype with a
proposed v0.7.0 whitepaper, and the Academy is an early beta.

### Phase I - Public Foundations and Digital Charter

The first phase is the work already underway: publish the Society's principles,
make their assumptions inspectable, test narrow implementations, and develop the
identity, governance, privacy, security, and verification work required before
any integrated civic platform could responsibly operate.

Proposed capabilities include:

- **Decentralized Identity (DID):** Person-controlled credentials that can establish eligibility and accountability without exposing unnecessary personal data.
- **Living Knowledge Graph:** Charter articles, discourse, amendments, evidence, authorship, and rationale connected in an inspectable public record.
- **Deliberation Interface:** Structured discussion with machine-assisted summarization intended to preserve dissent while making complex decisions understandable.
- **Purpose Economy Layer:** Budget-capped rewards for verified educational or civic contribution, with public parameters and safeguards against status becoming a payout formula.

### Phase II - Local Practice and Federation

If the digital foundations prove trustworthy, the Society could support local
learning, care, and coordination spaces shaped by the communities using them.
Possible forms include permaculture work, technical literacy, philosophy circles,
maker spaces, mutual aid, and restorative practice.

Proposed mechanics include:

- **Civic Pods:** Small, semi-autonomous groups that deliberate locally while remaining connected to shared safeguards.
- **Refactoring Cycles:** Regular public review of what worked, what failed, and what should change.
- **Education and Repair First:** Harm is met first with stabilization, understanding, learning, de-escalation, and repair. Immediate threats may still require the minimum effective intervention, with review and clear limits.

### Phase III - Distributed Infrastructure

If the Ark network or comparable infrastructure becomes viable, it could support
distributed education, civic records, ecological data, and local coordination
without placing every community under one technical or institutional point of
failure.

Proposed directions include:

- **Cognitive Mesh:** Devices, hubs, people, and machine systems contribute bandwidth, storage, or processing under understandable rules and meaningful consent.
- **Learning and Repair First:** Governance prioritizes feedback, education, restoration, and minimum necessary intervention while remaining accountable to applicable law and the Dignity Constraint.
- **Purpose-Aligned Economics:** Transparent, bounded mechanisms fund work that advances education, sustainability, care, and public well-being.
- **Ecological Participation:** Sensors and models help living systems enter human decisions as represented interests rather than resources without standing.

### Working Commitments

The project begins from several commitments that must remain open to evidence,
criticism, and revision:

1. **Dignity constrains power.** No person or conscious being becomes disposable because of status, capacity, conformity, or usefulness.
2. **Interdependence is unavoidable.** The practical question is whether it is organized through reciprocity and care or through dependence and domination.
3. **Stabilization, education, and repair should precede coercion.** Some immediate threats still require intervention, but force must be necessary, proportionate, limited, and reviewable.
4. **Progress must be observable.** Reduced suffering, expanded agency, ecological repair, accessible knowledge, and accountable power matter more than ideological purity or institutional self-preservation.

This is a proposal for an ecosystem of understanding and participation. It is
not presently a government, public authority, benefit program, or operating
monetary system.
